1. Porosity Calculator

φ = (Vp / Vb) × 100

2. Permeability (Darcy's Law)

k = (q × μ × L) / (A × ΔP)

3. STOIIP - Oil In Place

STOIIP = 7758 × A × h × φ × (1-Sw) / Bo

4. Formation Volume Factor

Bo = Vr / Vs

5. Oil Saturation

So = 1 - Sw

6. Recovery Factor

RF = (Np / STOIIP) × 100
