How Much is NYSC Allowance in 2026? Complete Breakdown (FG, State & PPA)

Last updated: October 9, 2026 | By Godstime Blog

One of the biggest questions every Prospective Corps Member (PCM) asks before going to camp is: "How much is NYSC allowance in 2026?" Will it be enough to survive the service year?

In this article, we will break down everything about NYSC allowance in 2026 - the Federal Government allawee, state government allowance, and PPA allowance. You will know exactly how much you will earn every month.

NYSC Federal Allowance in 2026 - How Much FG Pays

The Federal Government of Nigeria is the main body responsible for paying corps members. As of 2026, the official NYSC allowance paid by the Federal Government is ₦77,000 per month.

This amount was increased from ₦33,000 in 2024 after the new national minimum wage of ₦70,000 was approved. The NYSC Director General confirmed that corps members' allowance was reviewed to ₦77,000 to help corpers cope with economic realities.

Key things to know about FG allowance:

  • It is paid to ALL corps members regardless of state
  • Payment comes directly from NYSC, not your PPA
  • It is usually paid between 25th and 30th of every month
  • You must do monthly clearance to receive it
  • During orientation camp (3 weeks), you also get additional allowances like bicycle allowance of ₦2,500, transport allowance etc.

NYSC State Allowance 2026 - Which States Pay Corpers?

Apart from the ₦77,000 from Federal Government, some states in Nigeria pay extra allowance to corps members serving in their state. This is called State Allawee. However, not all states pay, and the amount differs.

This is one of the reasons many corpers pray to be posted to states like Lagos, Anambra, Akwa Ibom etc.

States That Pay High Allowance (2026)

  • Lagos State: Lagos is the highest paying state. They pay ₦20,000 to corpers in ministries and up to ₦50,000 to corpers in some special placements. Lagos also pays ₦10,000 extra for corpers serving in public schools.
  • Anambra State: Anambra pays ₦10,000 monthly to all corps members. Gov Soludo increased it from ₦8,000. Anambra is known for prompt payment.
  • Bayelsa State: Bayelsa pays between ₦6,000 to ₦10,000 depending on your LGA. They are also consistent.
  • Delta State: Delta pays ₦5,000 monthly. Sometimes they owe but they pay.
  • Akwa Ibom State: Akwa Ibom pays ₦5,000 to corps members. Very consistent state.
  • Enugu State: Enugu pays ₦10,000 to corps members serving in the state.
  • Sokoto State: Sokoto pays ₦5,000 plus accommodation for some corpers.
  • Kano State: Kano pays ₦5,000 to corpers in the state.
  • Jigawa State: Jigawa pays ₦5,000 - ₦10,000.
  • Taraba State: Taraba pays ₦8,000.

States That Do NOT Pay State Allowance

Unfortunately, many states do not pay any state allowance. These include: FCT Abuja, Oyo, Ogun, Osun, Ekiti, Rivers, Imo, Abia, Ebonyi, Borno, Yobe, Zamfara, Niger, Kogi, and many others. If you are posted to these states, you will only depend on FG ₦77k and your PPA.

Important: State allowance list changes. Some governors stop payment when they leave office. Always confirm from serving corpers.

PPA Allowance - How Much Your PPA Will Pay You

Your Place of Primary Assignment (PPA) is where you work during your service year. Your PPA can also pay you extra allowance. This is not compulsory, but many PPAs pay.

How Much PPA Pays in 2026

  • Private Secondary Schools: ₦10,000 - ₦40,000 per month. They pay the highest because they need teachers.
  • Government Schools (Public Schools): ₦5,000 - ₦15,000. Some don't pay at all.
  • Federal Ministries/State Ministries: Many don't pay. Some pay ₦5,000 - ₦10,000.
  • Local Government Secretariats: Usually ₦3,000 - ₦7,000.
  • Private Companies / Banks / Oil Companies: This is jackpot! Banks pay ₦30,000 - ₦80,000. Some private companies pay ₦50,000 - ₦100,000 if you are lucky.
  • NYSC PPA (Secretariat): If you are posted to NYSC secretariat, you may get little or no extra pay.

Tip: Before you accept PPA, ask serving corpers how much that PPA pays. You can reject PPA if you have genuine reason and request new posting from your LGI.

Total NYSC Allowance You Can Earn Per Month in 2026 (Calculation)

Let's do real calculation so you know what to expect:

Scenario 1 - Lucky Corper in Lagos posted to Bank:
FG ₦77,000 + Lagos State ₦20,000 + Bank PPA ₦60,000 = ₦157,000 per month

Scenario 2 - Corper in Anambra in Private School:
FG ₦77,000 + Anambra ₦10,000 + Private School ₦20,000 = ₦107,000 per month

Scenario 3 - Average Corper in Oyo (no state allowance) in Govt School:
FG ₦77,000 + State ₦0 + Govt School ₦5,000 = ₦82,000 per month

So as you can see, your total allowance depends on your state and PPA. That is why your posting matters a lot!

When Does NYSC Pay Allowance? Payment Date

NYSC FG allowance is paid monthly. It is usually paid from 25th to 30th of each month. Sometimes it delays to first week of next month due to network or budget issues.

You will start receiving allowance from the month you entered camp. For example, if Batch B 2026 camp starts in August, you will get August allowance in August/September.

How to Ensure You Receive Your Allowance Every Month

Many corpers miss allowance because of small mistakes. Avoid these:

  1. Do Monthly Clearance: Every month, go to your LGA for biometric clearance. If you miss clearance, you won't be paid.
  2. Monthly CDS: Attend Community Development Service (CDS) every week. If you miss, your LGI can block your allowance.
  3. Keep Your Account Active: Make sure the bank account you provided during registration is still active. Don't close it.
  4. Don't Travel Without Permission: If you travel without NYSC permission and you are absent from PPA, you may not get paid.

Does NYSC Pay Allowance During Orientation Camp?

Yes. During the 3 weeks orientation camp, you will be paid. You will get:

  • Bicycle allowance: ₦2,500
  • Transport allowance to camp: Based on distance
  • Other small camp allowances

Your first full ₦77,000 will come after camp.

Frequently Asked Questions About NYSC Allowance 2026

Q: Is NYSC allowance 77k true?
A: Yes. As of 2026, NYSC DG confirmed ₦77,000 is official FG allowance.

Q: Do NYSC pay corpers during strike?
A: Yes, if you are still in service and doing clearance, you will be paid even if your PPA is on strike.

Q: Can NYSC allowance be increased again?
A: Possibly. If minimum wage increases again, NYSC allowance may also increase.

Conclusion: In 2026, you should expect at least ₦77,000 monthly from FG. With state and PPA, you can earn over ₦100,000. Manage your money wisely during service year and try to save for after NYSC.
