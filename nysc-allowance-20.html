NYSC Allowance 2026: How Much FG Pays Corpers Now

FG allawee, state allowance, PPA allowance and when you go get am.

N77,000 / Month

FG has increased NYSC allowance to N77,000 per month in 2026.

How Much is NYSC Allowance in 2026?

  • FG Allowance (Allawee): N77,000 monthly
  • State Allowance: N5,000 - N30,000 depending on state
  • PPA Allowance: N5,000 - N20,000 (if your PPA pays)

Total monthly: N87,000 to N120,000 if you serve in a paying state.

When Does NYSC Pay Allowance?

NYSC pays between 25th and 30th of every month.

NYSC PPA Allowance

PPA allowance is not compulsory. Private schools pay N10k-N20k, companies pay N20k-N40k.

FAQs

Q: Is NYSC allowance N77,000 in 2026?
A: Yes, FG pays N77,000.

Q: Does Lagos pay extra?
A: Yes, Lagos pays N15k-N20k extra.

Q: Do all PPAs pay?
A: No, only some.
