What to Take to NYSC Camp 2026: Complete List of Requirements for Batch B

Last updated: October 9, 2026 | By Godstime Blog

You have seen your call-up letter and you are excited for NYSC camp! But the big question is: What should I take to NYSC orientation camp in 2026? If you forget important items, you will buy them at expensive prices inside camp.

In this guide, I will give you complete list of NYSC camp requirements and personal things you must take to survive 21 days in camp.

A. Compulsory Documents - You Won't Enter Camp Without These!

These documents are MANDATORY. Without them, they will send you home from camp gate:

  1. NYSC Call-up Letter: Print it in COLORED. Print 5 copies. This is your entry ticket. You printed from your dashboard.
  2. NYSC Green Card: Print colored. 5 copies. This is your registration slip.
  3. Final Year School ID Card: Original and 5 photocopies. If you lost it, go with your departmental ID or get affidavit.
  4. Degree/HND Certificate or Statement of Result: Original and 5 photocopies. The one you used for registration.
  5. Medical Certificate / Fitness Certificate: Go to any government hospital and get fitness certificate. Some camps ask for it, some don't, but take it to be safe.
  6. Passport Photographs: At least 10-15 copies with white background. You will need many in camp.
  7. COVID-19 Vaccination Card (optional now): In 2022/2023 they asked, but now not compulsory. Take it if you have.

Tip: Put all documents inside a clear file bag and waterproof file to avoid rain damage.

B. NYSC White Clothes Requirements

NYSC camp is all about white. You will wear white from morning to night:

  • White T-shirts: Take at least 5-7 white round-neck T-shirts. You will sweat a lot during parade and drills.
  • White Shorts: 3-4 white shorts. NYSC official one is white. Buy quality one that is not transparent.
  • White Canvas / Tennis Shoes: 1 or 2 pairs. Buy white rubber canvas. Don't buy expensive designer, it will get dirty.
  • White Socks: 5 pairs of white socks. Thick ones. You march every day.
  • White Sneakers for drills: Some camps insist on white, some allow any color.

You will be given 2 NYSC crested vests, 1 pair of khaki trousers, 1 jungle boot, 1 NYSC cap and belt inside camp. But the white quality given is poor, so take extra white from home.

C. Other Clothes to Take

  • Khaki trousers: They will give you one, but you can take extra plain khaki trouser if you have. You wear it on official days.
  • Plain Casual Wear: For evenings, you need 3-4 casual clothes for when you go to Mammy Market.
  • Underwear and Singlets: Take enough - at least 7 pants, 5 singlets. You may not wash everyday.
  • Towels: 2 towels, one big and one small.
  • Bedspread and Pillowcase: You will be given a thin mattress. Take your own bedsheet and pillowcase. Mattress is hard.
  • Mufti for Camp Activities: For social nights and Sundays.

D. Toiletries and Personal Care

  • Bathing soap and sponge
  • Toothpaste and toothbrush
  • Body cream, face cream
  • Roll-on and perfume (small one)
  • Tissue paper - at least 2 rolls
  • Detergent - small sachets to wash your whites
  • Bucket - you can buy bucket in camp mammy market for ₦1000-₦1500, but you can take small one
  • Antiseptic like Dettol
  • Shaving stick and small mirror

E. Important Personal Items for NYSC Camp 2026

  • Waist Bag / Fanny Pack: Very important! To keep your phone, money, ID card during parade. Don't carry handbag.
  • Padlock: 2 small padlocks for your box and locker. Theft is common in camp hostels.
  • Power Bank: At least 20,000mAh or 30,000mAh. Light in camp is not stable and many people struggle to charge. Fully charge it before going.
  • Phone Charger and Extension Cord: Take multi-socket extension. You will be God to many people.
  • Cash: Take at least ₦40,000 - ₦60,000 cash. ATM inside camp often doesn't work and POS charges ₦300-₦500 per transaction. Break it into ₦500 and ₦1000 notes.
  • Water Bottle / Flask: You will parade under sun. Stay hydrated.
  • Sunglasses and Face Cap: Sun in camp is brutal, especially in northern states.
  • Bible/Quran and Notebook + Pen: For lectures and religious activities.

F. Food and Snacks

Camp food is not the best. Take some provisions:

  • Biscuits, groundnut, chin-chin
  • Milk, milo, sugar in small containers
  • Garri, but don't take much - you can buy in mammy
  • Small electric kettle (hidden) - some hostels allow it for tea, but officially banned. Many corpers still take it.

G. What NOT to Take to NYSC Camp

Don't take these or you will have problem at gate:

  • Laptop - not needed, can be stolen
  • Expensive phones - take 1 good phone, not 2 expensive ones
  • Excessive jewelry or expensive clothes
  • Cars - you can't drive in camp
  • Knives, weapons, hard drugs - you will be decamped

NYSC Camp Packing Tips for 2026 Batch B

  1. Don't overpack - you will be carrying your load under sun. Pack smart in one big Ghana Must Go or box.
  2. Label all your white clothes with your name - clothes steal common.
  3. Go to camp early on first day (before 12pm) to avoid long registration queue.
  4. Carry small handbag with documents separate from main box.
  5. Make photocopies of everything at least 5 copies.

Conclusion: If you pack everything in this list, you will enjoy your 3 weeks orientation camp without stress. Many people forget padlock, power bank, white socks and regret it. Use this checklist to pack.
