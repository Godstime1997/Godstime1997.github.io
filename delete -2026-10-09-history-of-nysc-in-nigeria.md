# The History of NYSC in Nigeria: The Khaki That United A Broken Nation

After the Nigerian Civil War ended in 1970, Nigeria was broken. There was hatred and distrust. General Yakubu Gowon needed something to bring Nigerian youths together.

On 22nd May 1973, he created NYSC by Decree No. 24.

## Chapter 1: The Beginning (1973)

The first set was 2,000 corpers from 7 universities. They wore khaki and served far from home to learn other cultures.

## Chapter 2: The Purpose

NYSC was built on 3 pillars: Unity, Service and Patriotism. The motto is "Service and Humility".

## Chapter 3: How It Has Changed

From 1973 till today:
- 1973: Started with 2,000
- 1980: Became compulsory for all graduates
- 1993: Decree 51 replaced Decree 24
- 2024: Monthly allawee increased to N77,000
- 2026: Over 5 million graduates have served.

## Chapter 4: The NYSC Experience Today

Orientation camp for 3 weeks, PPA, CDS, and POP. Every Nigerian graduate still wears the khaki with pride.

## Chapter 5: Why It Still Matters

Despite calls to scrap it, NYSC remains the only program that forces an Igbo boy to live in Sokoto and a Hausa girl to live in Onitsha. That is true unity.
