NYSC Call-Up Letter 2026: How to Print & What to Do After

Last updated: October 9, 2026 | By Godstime NYSC Info

Your NYSC Call-Up Letter is the most important document for Batch A, B, C 2026. Without it, you cannot enter orientation camp. Here is complete guide on how to print it, what it contains, and what to do after.

Latest: NYSC 2026 Batch B Call-Up Letter will be available 3 days before camp opens. Keep checking dashboard.

What is NYSC Call-Up Letter?

Call-up letter is official letter from NYSC Director General inviting you for national service. It contains:

  • Your full name and Call-up Number
  • State of deployment (where you will serve)
  • Orientation camp address
  • Camp resumption date
  • Your course of study and institution
  • Officer-in-charge stamp

How to Print NYSC Call-Up Letter 2026 (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Go to NYSC Portal

Visit portal.nysc.org.ng and login with your email and password you used for registration.

Step 2: Click Print Call-Up Letter

On your dashboard, you will see "Print Call-Up Letter" button. Click it.

Step 3: Print and Make Copies

Print in colored or black and white. Make at least 4 photocopies + laminate the original? No, do NOT laminate original.

Warning: Print only from cyber cafe with good printer. Blurry call-up letter will be rejected at camp gate.

Sample of NYSC Call-Up Letter (What it looks like)

It contains NYSC logo, your photo, DG signature, and this format:

"I am pleased to inform you that you have been called up for National Service..."

Then your state, e.g., "You have been deployed to KANO State..."

What To Do After Printing Call-Up Letter

  1. Check your state: Start researching cost of living, weather, camp location.
  2. Print Green Card: Your NYSC Green Card (Registration slip) is also required at camp.
  3. Prepare documents: Go to our article on What to Take to NYSC Camp 2026
  4. Do not travel late: Camp gate closes 12am on second day. Late comers are decamped.

Common Issues Printing Call-Up Letter

1. "Call-Up Letter not yet available"

Means NYSC has not released for your stream. Wait 24-48 hours. Check our Registration Guide.

2. "Payment not successful"

You must pay N3,000 for online printing. If you chose not to pay during registration, you must collect from school. Pay online for instant access.

3. Wrong State or Name Error

If your name has error, go to camp with affidavit. State cannot be changed except on medical/marital grounds after camp.

Can I Change My State of Deployment?

After printing call-up letter, you CANNOT change state online. You can only apply for relocation inside camp on 3 grounds:

  • Medical (with strong medical report)
  • Marital (for married women with proof)
  • Security threat (rarely approved)

Final Checklist Before Going to Camp

  • ✅ Call-Up Letter (original + 4 copies)
  • ✅ Green Card (original + 4 copies)
  • ✅ Degree/HND Certificate
  • ✅ School ID + Final Year ID
  • ✅ Read full list: Camp Requirements List 2026

Conclusion

Print your NYSC Call-Up Letter early, make copies, and prepare for camp. Do not wait till last day - cyber cafes will be crowded and network may be bad.
