NYSC Registration Guide 2026 Batch B - Step by Step

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Are you ready for NYSC 2026 Batch B mobilization? This guide will show you everything you need to register successfully on the NYSC portal without mistakes.

Requirements for NYSC Registration 2026

  • Valid email address and phone number
  • Matriculation number
  • JAMB Registration number
  • Passport photograph (white background)
  • Final year clearance
  • Degree/HND certificate or Statement of Result

Step by Step NYSC Registration Process

Step 1: Visit NYSC Portal

Go to portal.nysc.org.ng and click on "Mobilization Batch B 2026"

Step 2: Fresh Registration

If you have never registered before, click on "Fresh Registration". If you are revalidating, click revalidation.

Step 3: Fill Personal Details

Enter your name as it appears on your JAMB, date of birth, and other personal info. Make sure it matches your school records!

Step 4: Upload Documents

Upload your passport photo, school ID, and required certificates. Photo must be clear, white background.

Step 5: Biometric Capture

You will need thumbprint. Use cyber cafe with biometric scanner or use NYSC mobile app.

Step 6: Preview and Submit

Check everything carefully! NYSC does not allow correction of name and date of birth after submission.

Step 7: Print Green Card

After submission, print your Green Card and payment slip. This is your evidence of registration.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong date of birth - can disqualify you
  • Blurry passport photo
  • Name different from JAMB/School record
  • Using someone else's phone number

When is NYSC 2026 Batch B Registration Closing?

NYSC 2026 Batch B registration usually closes 2 weeks after opening. Register early to avoid last minute network issues.

Next: After registration, check your name on NYSC Senate List and wait for Call-up Letter.
