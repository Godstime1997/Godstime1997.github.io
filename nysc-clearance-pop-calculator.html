NYSC Monthly Clearance Rules - No Miss Am

1. Clearance is every month, usually last week. If you miss am, NYSC go hold your allawee.

2. Go with your PPA letter, sign, collect your clearance slip.

3. CDS is every Wednesday. Miss 3 CDS = query / extension.

4. Final clearance is 1 week before POP. Do am early.

⚠️ Extension: If you miss clearance, travel without permission, or miss CDS, NYSC fit extend your service by 21 days - 3 months. No risk am.
