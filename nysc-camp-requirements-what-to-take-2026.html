NYSC Camp Requirements 2026: What to Take to Orientation Camp

Last updated: October 9, 2026 | By Godstime

Going to NYSC camp 2026? Here is the complete list of NYSC camp requirements you MUST take. If you forget one, you will be sent back home.

Important: Camp gate closes 12am second day. Come early!

1. Documents Required (Compulsory)

  • Call-Up Letter: Original + 4 photocopies. DO NOT laminate. How to Print Call-Up Letter
  • Green Card: Original + 4 photocopies. Print in colour. Do NOT laminate
  • Degree/HND Statement of Result: Original + 4 photocopies
  • School ID Card: Original + 2 photocopies (Final year ID)
  • Passport Photographs: 8 copies, white background
  • Medical Fitness Certificate: From government hospital
  • NIN Slip
Warning: Laminated Call-Up Letter or Green Card will be rejected at camp!

2. Personal Items to Pack

  • White t-shirts, white shorts, white socks (at least 5)
  • White canvas / sneakers
  • Bathroom slippers, towels, toiletries
  • Bucket, cutlery, padlock
  • Cash - at least N20,000 to N30,000 (ATM queue is long)
  • Power bank, extension, torchlight

3. What NOT to Take

  • Do not take car, expensive jewelry
  • Do not take fake documents - you will be arrested

Final Checklist

  • ✅ All documents in a file
  • ✅ White wears ready
  • ✅ Cash
  • ✅ Have you checked NYSC Allowance 2026?
  • ✅ Check Registration Guide
