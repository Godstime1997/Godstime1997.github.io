NYSC PPA Posting 2026

How to Check Where You Are Posted & What To Do Next

NYSC PPA posting 2026 is the final posting after orientation camp. If you are in Batch A, B or C Stream 1 or 2, this guide shows you how to check your PPA, collect your posting letter and what to do if you want to reject or change it.

🔥 QUICK ANSWER: Your PPA posting letter is given to you on the last day of NYSC camp. You can also check online via portal.nysc.org.ng → Login → PPA Posting Letter. Your PPA is where you will work for 11 months.

What is NYSC PPA?

PPA means Place of Primary Assignment. It is where NYSC will post you to work after the 21-day orientation camp.

Your PPA can be:

  • School (primary/secondary)
  • Government ministry or agency
  • Local Government Secretariat
  • Private company (if approved)
  • Clinic / Hospital (for medical corpers)

When Will NYSC Release PPA Posting 2026?

PPA posting is released on the last day of orientation camp (Day 21).

How to Check NYSC PPA Posting 2026

Method 1: On Camp (Official)

  1. On Day 20/21, your LGI will call you
  2. Go to the posting hall with your ID card
  3. Collect your PPA posting letter
  4. The letter contains: Your PPA name, Address, LGA, and Reporting date

Method 2: Online (After Camp)

  1. Visit portal.nysc.org.ng
  2. Login with email and password
  3. Click "PPA Posting Letter"
  4. Download and print

Sample of NYSC PPA Posting Letter

Your letter will contain:

  • Your name and call-up number
  • State code
  • PPA name and address
  • LGA and Zone
  • Instructions to report within 48 hours

What To Do After Getting PPA Letter?

  1. Report to your LGA: Go to your LGA secretariat first for documentation
  2. Report to your PPA: Take posting letter to your PPA within 2 days
  3. Acceptance/Rejection: Your PPA boss will sign acceptance letter
  4. Return acceptance to LGI: Submit signed copy to your Local Government Inspector

Can I Reject My PPA? Yes But...

Yes, you can reject if:

  • PPA is too far from town
  • PPA does not provide accommodation
  • PPA is not your field

How to reject: Politely tell your PPA you have health issue or accommodation problem. They will write "REJECTED" on your letter. Take it back to LGI who will re-post you to another PPA in same LGA.

Note: You cannot reject more than once officially. Be wise.

NYSC Redeployment / Relocation 2026

If you want to change state entirely, you need redeployment (relocation) on medical or marital grounds.

How to apply:

  1. After camp, write application letter to State Coordinator through your LGI
  2. Attach evidence: Medical report, Marriage certificate, etc.
  3. Submit at NYSC Secretariat
  4. Wait 2-4 weeks for approval

NYSC PPA Posting - Important Tips

  • Never pay to get good PPA - it's random
  • Always report within 48 hours or you may be queried
  • Keep photocopies of all letters
  • Build good relationship with your PPA boss - they sign your monthly clearance
  • Your PPA determines your CDS day and allowance bonus (some PPAs pay N5k - N30k extra)

FAQ - NYSC PPA Posting 2026

Q: Will NYSC pay me if I reject PPA?
A: Yes, if LGI re-posts you quickly. No allowance delay.

Q: Can I choose my PPA?
A: Unofficially yes if you know someone at secretariat before last day of camp.

Q: How much do PPA pay?
A: Federal PPA: N0 extra. State: N5k-N10k. Private schools/companies: N15k-N40k.
