NYSC Senate List 2026: How to Check Your Name for Batch A, B, C

Published: May 12, 2026 • 5 mins read • By Godstime

Quick Summary: Senate List is official list of graduates approved by your school for NYSC. If your name is not on Senate List, you cannot register on NYSC portal. Check at portal.nysc.org.ng

What is NYSC Senate List?

Senate List is compiled by your institution. Only graduates whose school Senate approved will see their name. It confirms you truly graduated and qualified for NYSC.

How to Check NYSC Senate List 2026 (Step by Step)

Step 1: Visit https://portal.nysc.org.ng
Step 2: Click "Check Senate List or its Equivalents"
Step 3: Select your Institution
Step 4: Enter Matric Number, Surname, Date of Birth
Step 5: Click Search. If your name appears, you are approved!

What Does "No Record Found" Mean?

This means your school has NOT yet uploaded your name, or there is error in Matric Number, or you are not cleared. 90% time it is because Senate List not yet released for your batch.

What To Do If Name Not On Senate List

1. Go to Student Affairs / DSA in your school immediately
2. Confirm you did final clearance and paid all fees
3. Check JAMB Matriculation List - your name must be there too
4. Ask if your department already sent list to Senate
5. For foreign graduates, ensure you evaluated certificate

Pro Tip: Always check JAMB Matriculation List first at https://portal.jamb.gov.ng/efacility_/CheckMatriculationList - If not on JAMB list, you won't be on NYSC Senate List.
