📘 NYSC Mobilization Guide 2026 - NDU Petroleum Engineering

1. Who is Eligible? All NDU graduates below 30 years at time of graduation. Your DOB on NDU portal must match JAMB.

2. Senate List: After final year clearance, NDU ICT uploads your name. Check at portal.nysc.org.ng → Senate List. Search with matric UG/19/0892 format.

3. Required Documents for Registration:
• NDU Statement of Result / Certificate
• JAMB Admission Letter & Result Slip
• Passport photo (white background)
• Valid Email & Phone

4. For PET Department: Supervisor must sign project clearance. Ensure no carryover, departmental dues cleared, DSA clearance done at Wilberforce Island.

5. Common Problems & Fix:
• Name mismatch → Go to Exams & Records, Amassoma
• DOB error → Court Affidavit + JAMB correction
• Not on Senate List → Contact Faculty Officer / DSA

6. Batch: NDU usually goes Batch B Stream II or Batch C. Stay updated on NYSC official Instagram.
