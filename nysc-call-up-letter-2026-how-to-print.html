NYSC Call-Up Letter 2026: How to Print & What to Do After

Last updated: October 9, 2026 | By Godstime

Your NYSC Call-Up Letter is the most important document for Batch A, B, C 2026.

Latest: Batch B Call-Up Letter will be available 3 days before camp opens.

What is NYSC Call-Up Letter?

  • Your full name and Call-up Number
  • State of deployment
  • Orientation camp address
  • Camp resumption date

How to Print

1. Visit portal.nysc.org.ng
2. Click "Print Call-Up Letter"
3. Print in colored, make 4 copies - Do NOT laminate

Warning: Blurry call-up letter will be rejected at camp gate.

What To Do After Printing

  1. Check your state
  2. Print Green Card
  3. Prepare documents: NYSC Camp Requirements 2026
  4. Do not travel late

Final Checklist

  • ✅ Call-Up Letter (original + 4 copies)
  • ✅ Green Card (original + 4 copies)
  • ✅ Degree/HND Certificate
  • ✅ Full list: Camp Requirements List
