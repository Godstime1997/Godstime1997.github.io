NYSC Call-Up Letter 2026: How to Print & What to Do After

Last updated: October 9, 2026 | By Godstime

Your NYSC Call-Up Letter is the most important document for Batch A, B, C 2026. Without it, you cannot enter orientation camp.

Latest: NYSC 2026 Batch B Call-Up Letter will be available 3 days before camp opens.

What is NYSC Call-Up Letter?

  • Your full name and Call-up Number
  • State of deployment
  • Orientation camp address
  • Camp resumption date

How to Print NYSC Call-Up Letter 2026

1. Visit portal.nysc.org.ng and login
2. Click "Print Call-Up Letter"
3. Print in colored, make 4 copies - Do NOT laminate original

Warning: Blurry call-up letter will be rejected at camp gate.

What To Do After Printing

  1. Check your state
  2. Print Green Card
  3. Prepare documents: Camp Requirements 2026
  4. Do not travel late

Common Issues

"Call-Up Letter not yet available" - Wait 24-48 hours. Check Registration Guide.

"Payment not successful" - You must pay N3,000 for online printing.

Final Checklist Before Going to Camp

  • ✅ Call-Up Letter (original + 4 copies)
  • ✅ Green Card (original + 4 copies)
  • ✅ Degree/HND Certificate
  • ✅ Read full list: Camp Requirements List 2026
