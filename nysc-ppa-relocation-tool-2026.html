How To Relocate After Camp - Real Steps

1. Health: Go camp clinic, get medical report, apply for relocation with proof. Highest approval.

2. Marriage: Marriage certificate + husband's ID + proof of residence. Very high approval for women.

4. Work: Hardest. Need PPA rejection letter + new PPA acceptance letter from desired state.
