NYSC Allowance 2026: How Much FG Pays Corpers Allowance 2026: How Much FG Pays Corpers Now

Updated: October 12, 2026

FG has increased NYSC allowance to N77,000 per month in 2026. This is the official Federal Government allawee paid to all corps members nationwide.

How Much is NYSC Allowance in 2026?

  • FG Allowance (Allawee): N77,000 monthly
  • State Allowance: N5,000 - N30,000 depending on state
  • PPA Allowance: N5,000 - N20,000 (if your PPA pays)

Total you can get monthly: N87,000 to N120,000 if you serve in a paying state.

When Does NYSC Pay Allowance?

NYSC pays between 25th and 30th of every month.

NYSC PPA Allowance

PPA allowance is not compulsory. Private schools pay N10k-N20k, companies pay N20k-N40k.
